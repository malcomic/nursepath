You do not need to “know” every item cold. You need a **reliable way to delete what cannot be right**. Elimination turns four scary options into a manageable choice — and it works across multiple choice, SATA, and many NGN formats.

## The Elimination Funnel

### Step 1 — Cut absolute losers

Remove options that are:

- **Unsafe** (ignore airway, leave unstable client, break sterile technique without indication)
- **Out of scope** (UAP doing initial teaching / assessment reserved for RN)
- **Irrelevant** to the stem’s actual ask
- **Opposite** of the verb (doing something when asked what to *avoid*)

### Step 2 — Match survivors to the stem

Re-read the verb: assess, intervene, evaluate, teach, prioritize, delegate.

Ask: which remaining option answers *that* verb for *this* client?

### Step 3 — Compare the final two

Prefer the option that is:

- More **immediate** when the client is unstable
- More **specific** to the problem (not vaguely helpful)
- More **assessment-first** when data is missing — unless crisis demands action

### Step 4 — Commit

Pick and move. Endless second-guessing burns time — see [exam-day pacing](/blog/nclex-exam-day-time-management).

## Distractor Patterns to Recognize

| Pattern | Example vibe | Why it’s wrong |
|---------|--------------|----------------|
| Nice nurse | Extra comfort while ABC fails | Wrong priority |
| Familiar fact | True statement, wrong disease | Irrelevant |
| Absolute word misuse | Always / never used carelessly | Overgeneralized |
| Delegation trap | Assigning assessment to UAP | Scope |
| Early intervention | Treating before assessing when assess is needed | Process order |
| Delay care | “Wait and document” in deterioration | Unsafe |

## Mini Example

**Stem:** Client with new stridor after thyroidectomy. What should the nurse do first?

| Option | Keep? |
|--------|-------|
| Offer ice chips for sore throat | Cut — ignores airway threat |
| Check the dressing for bleeding only | Cut as *first* — airway first |
| Sit client up and prepare for airway support / notify provider per protocol | Keep |
| Teach incision care for discharge tomorrow | Cut — wrong time |

## SATA and NGN Twist

For [SATA](/blog/how-to-answer-sata-questions-nclex) and [EMR](/blog/extended-multiple-response-nclex-strategy), elimination happens **per option**, not by hunting a count. For [bowtie](/blog/bowtie-questions-nclex-strategy) and [matrix](/blog/matrix-grid-nclex-strategy), eliminate anything that does not serve the priority problem or row context.

<Callout title="Study habit">
When reviewing rationales, write one line: “I should have eliminated ___ because ___.” That builds the funnel faster than re-reading content alone.
</Callout>

## Practice Plan

- 15 items daily: force yourself to cross out losers before selecting
- Weekly: only review items you guessed between two options
- Tie judgment to the [CJMM](/blog/how-cjmm-works-nclex) so elimination stays clinical, not superstitious
