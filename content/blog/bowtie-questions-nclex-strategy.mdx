Bowtie items look intimidating because of the shape. Mentally, they are simple: **name the priority problem in the center**, then choose **actions** and **parameters to monitor** that belong to *that* problem — not every helpful nursing action in the universe.

## Bowtie Anatomy

  src="/blog/bowtie-anatomy.svg"

  width={800}

Typical layout:

- **Left:** Actions to take (interventions)
- **Center:** Potential condition / complication / problem
- **Right:** Parameters to monitor (assessments, labs, signs)

## The Center-First Method

1. **Read the scenario** and list top cues.
2. **Choose the center** — the most likely *or* most dangerous problem the cues support.
3. **Select left-side actions** that treat or prevent that center problem safely.
4. **Select right-side monitors** that show improvement or deterioration of that same problem.
5. **Cross-check:** If an action or monitor belongs to a *different* diagnosis, drop it.

## Worked Mini-Example

**Cues:** Post-op day 1 hip replacement, sudden SOB, SpO₂ 88%, HR 128, anxiety, unilateral calf pain earlier today.

**Center (priority):** Pulmonary embolism (vs “anxiety” alone).

**Actions (examples of the type you’d seek):** Apply O₂, elevate HOB, notify provider / rapid response per protocol, stay with client — *not* “encourage ambulation now.”

**Monitors:** SpO₂, RR, HR, mental status, chest pain — *not* incision drainage as the primary PE monitor.

## Common Bowtie Mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Picking a vague center (“discomfort”) | Name the clinical threat |
| Mixing two problems across wings | One priority drives both sides |
| Selecting every safe action | Match to center only |
| Monitoring unrelated systems | Ask what proves the center is improving |

<Callout title="CJMM map">
Center ≈ Prioritize Hypotheses. Left ≈ Take Action. Right ≈ Evaluate Outcomes. See [How the CJMM works](/blog/how-cjmm-works-nclex).
</Callout>

## Drill Plan

- 5 bowtie items, three times a week
- After each: write one sentence justifying center, then one linking each selected wing option
